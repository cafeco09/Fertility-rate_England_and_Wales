Digital 2022: The United Kingdom

This page contains all the data, insights, and trends you need to help you understand how people in the United Kingdom use connected devices and services in 2022.

You’ll find our complete Digital 2022 report on the United Kingdom in the “full report” section below, but let’s start by taking a look at the essential headlines for digital adoption and use in the UK this year.

The United Kingdom’s population in 2022

The United Kingdom’s total population was 68.35 million in January 2022.

Data show that the UK’s population increased by 306 thousand (+0.4 percent) between 2021 and 2022.

50.6 percent of the United Kingdom’s population is female, while 49.4 percent of the population is male.

At the start of 2022, 84.4 percent of the United Kingdom’s population lived in urban centres, while 15.6 percent lived in rural areas.

Note: gender data are currently only available for “female” and “male”.

The United Kingdom’s population by age

The median age of the population in the United Kingdom is 40.8.

For additional context, here’s a look at how the population in the United Kingdom breaks down by age group:

  • 5.6 percent of the UK’s population is between the ages of 0 and 4.

  • 9.6 percent of the UK’s population is between the ages of 5 and 12.

  • 5.7 percent of the UK’s population is between the ages of 13 and 17.

  • 7.9 percent of the UK’s population is between the ages of 18 and 24.

  • 13.2 percent of the UK’s population is between the ages of 25 and 34.

  • 13.3 percent of the UK’s population is between the ages of 35 and 44.

  • 12.8 percent of the UK’s population is between the ages of 45 and 54.

  • 12.8 percent of the UK’s population is between the ages of 55 and 64.

  • 19.0 percent of the UK’s population is aged 65 and above.

Note: percentages may not sum to 100 percent due to rounding.

Internet use in the United Kingdom in 2022

There were 66.99 million internet users in the United Kingdom in January 2022.

The United Kingdom’s internet penetration rate stood at 98.0 percent of the total population at the start of 2022.

Kepios analysis indicates that internet users in the UK increased by 300 thousand (+0.4 percent) between 2021 and 2022.

For perspective, these user figures reveal that 1.37 million people in the UK did not use the internet at the start of 2022, meaning that 2.0 percent of the population remained offline at the beginning of the year.

However, issues relating to COVID-19 continue to impact research into internet adoption, so actual internet user figures may be higher than these published numbers suggest (see here for further details).

Internet connection speeds in the United Kingdom in 2022

Data published by Ookla indicate that internet users in the UK could have expected the following internet connection speeds at the start of 2022:

  • Median mobile internet connection speed via cellular networks: 47.00 Mbps.

  • Median fixed internet connection speed: 58.80 Mbps.

Ookla’s data reveals that the median mobile internet connection speed in the United Kingdom increased by 18.72 Mbps (+66.2 percent) in the twelve months to the start of 2022.

Meanwhile, Ookla’s data shows that fixed internet connection speeds in the UK increased by 12.94 Mbps (+28.2 percent) during the same period.

Social media statistics for the United Kingdom in 2022

There were 57.60 million social media users in the United Kingdom in January 2022.

The number of social media users in the UK at the start of 2022 was equivalent to 84.3 percent of the total population, but it’s important to note that social media users may not represent unique individuals (see our detailed notes on data to learn why).

Kepios analysis reveals that social media users in the United Kingdom increased by 4.6 million (+8.7 percent) between 2021 and 2022.

Facebook users in the United Kingdom in 2022

Data published in Meta’s advertising resources indicates that Facebook had 35.05 million users in the United Kingdom in early 2022.

However, Meta made important changes to the way its advertising resources report audience reach data at the end of 2021 – including making significant revisions to its base audience data for Facebook – so the figures shown here are not directly comparable with figures published in our previous reports.

To learn more about these changes, please see this article, as well as our comprehensive notes on data.

The company’s revised audience figures mean that Facebook’s ad reach in the UK was equivalent to 51.3 percent of the total population at the start of 2022.

However, Facebook restricts the use of its platform to people aged 13 and above, so it’s also worth highlighting that 60.5 percent of the “eligible” audience in the UK uses Facebook in 2022.

For additional context, Facebook’s ad reach in the United Kingdom was equivalent to 52.3 percent of the local internet user base (regardless of age) in January 2022.

At the start of 2022, 54.1 percent of Facebook’s ad audience in the UK was female, while 45.9 percent was male.

Note: Meta’s advertising resources only publish audience gender data for “female” and “male” users.

Mobile connections in the United Kingdom in 2022

Data from GSMA Intelligence shows that there were 71.84 million cellular mobile connections in the United Kingdom at the start of 2022.

However, note that many people around the world make use of more than one mobile connection – for example, they might have one connection for personal use, and another one for work – so it’s not unusual for mobile connection figures to significantly exceed figures for total population.

GSMA Intelligence’s numbers indicate that mobile connections in the UK were equivalent to 105 percent of the total population in January 2022.

The number of mobile connections in the United Kingdom increased by 2.6 million (+3.8 percent) between 2021 and 2022.
